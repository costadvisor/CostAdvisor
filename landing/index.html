05

05 · Negotiate · in the app: Negotiate

Walk in with the number, the floor and the answer.

The brief gives Aquaverde a target, a walk-away floor and the index evidence. Then it checks each claim Supplier A makes against what the index really did.

Call script Rolling outthe app's template, filled from the brief
  1. “We have modelled ferric chloride 40% bottom-up from published indexes. The defensible number is €291/t; your current price is €339/t, €48/t above that.”
  2. “We are ready to settle at €291/t. Below €252/t there is no margin left in the chain, so that is not a number we expect you to accept.”

Every figure comes from the engine, not from a language model.

Security details and the fine print

Questions buyers ask.

Straight answers. The longer version of each is on the Method and Security pages.

Why should I trust your numbers?

They come from public statistics and exchange benchmarks, not from suppliers. Every series names its source where one is documented, and every chart shows its data date.

Where does the index data come from? Is it licensed?

Mostly public statistics and exchange benchmarks: US BLS, World Bank, FRED, USGS, Eurostat, EIA, LME and others. If you already pay for a price agency, you can use your own index values.

How current is it?

Indexes are monthly, with history since January 2023; the current release runs to June 2026. Outlooks run six months, and flat carry-forwards are labelled. Exchange rates update daily from the ECB.

Will it cover my products?

The library holds 1,750 product cards across 25 families and 50 industries, and grows with each release. Anything missing can be modelled in the cost model builder.

Who checked the makers?

On 935 cards, makers of that exact product are verified on their own pages. Other cards say they are not yet verified.

Does my supplier see anything? Is my data safe?

No. Your prices, suppliers and strategies stay in your team's private workspace. The shared library is read-only, and nothing flows back into it.

How much work is setup?

Start from the library: one click per product gives you a cost model. Price lists and quotes come in as PDFs you confirm line by line; CSV and Excel cover the rest. No ERP integration is needed.

Will my supplier accept a should-cost argument?

The brief shows which indexes moved and by how much, with their sources, and checks the supplier's own claims against them. It never guesses the supplier's real costs.

How does this sit next to a price-agency subscription?

CostAdvisor does not replace a price agency. It turns index moves into your should-cost, your gap and your negotiation position, and you can use your own agency values.

Where does CostAdvisor use AI?

Research is AI-assisted and evidence-checked: sources named, makers verified on their own pages, no invented numbers. In the product, the figures in a brief come from the calculation engine, not from a language model. The AI features run on a model we host, and nothing goes to a third-party AI provider.
