The fine print, in plain language

How we handle your data, your IP, and your trust — no dark patterns.

Terms of Service

Use CostAdvisor to build and analyse should-cost models for your organisation's procurement. Redistribution, resale, or reverse-engineering is prohibited. It's a decision-support tool — we're not liable for commercial outcomes of the figures or briefs it generates.

Privacy & GDPR

Operated from the EU on the lawful basis of contract performance and legitimate interest. You can access, correct, export, or delete your data at any time. We never sell personal data.

Your Data, Your IP

Your cost models, uploaded data, and negotiation briefs stay your intellectual property. The platform, formulas, and UI are ours. Index data (ECB, EIA, Eurostat, FRED, World Bank) is provided for information — always validate before negotiating.

Residency & Retention

Data is stored on EU (Frankfurt) Postgres, encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). Account data is kept for your subscription term; on deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days.

Authentication & AI

Google OAuth for sign-in — we never store passwords. AI narratives run on a self-hosted model; no data is sent to OpenAI or any third-party AI provider.
